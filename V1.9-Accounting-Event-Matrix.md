## 1. Accounting Chart of Accounts

The minimal, complete, and unambiguous chart of accounts for the Business Management & POS System:

| Account Code | Account Name | Class | Normal Balance | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1010** | **Cash** | Asset | Debit | Physical cash, bank balances, and electronic payment accounts |
| **1100** | **Accounts Receivable** | Asset | Debit | Uncollected amounts owed by customers on posted sales |
| **1200** | **Inventory** | Asset | Debit | Total valuation of on-hand merchandise and raw/finished stock |
| **1300** | **Supplier Receivable** | Asset | Debit | Refund/credit amounts owed to the business by suppliers from cancelled/returned purchases |
| **2010** | **Accounts Payable** | Liability | Credit | Unpaid obligations owed to suppliers for received goods and freight |
| **2100** | **Customer Refund Liability** | Liability | Credit | Obligations owed to customers for cancelled or returned paid sales pending cash disbursement |
| **3010** | **Sales Revenue** | Equity (P&L) | Credit | Gross revenue earned from posted sales |
| **3020** | **Other Income** | Equity (P&L) | Credit | Non-sales income recorded via manual finance entries |
| **4010** | **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)** | Equity (P&L) | Debit | Cost basis of inventory sold, snapshotted at post time |
| **5010** | **Operating Expenses** | Equity (P&L) | Debit | Operational, administrative, and general expenses recorded manually |
| **6000** | **Owner's Equity** | Equity | Credit | Initial capital, owner injections, and cumulative retained earnings |

## 2. Core Accounting Principles & PRD Alignments

1. **Costing Engine — Moving Average Cost (PRD §18.1):**
   - V1 strictly uses **Moving Average Costing**, NOT FIFO.
   - Upon purchase receipt of $Q_{\text{recv}}$ units at unit price $P_{\text{recv}}$ with allocated freight $S_{\text{recv}}$:
     $$\text{New Unit Cost} = \frac{(Q_{\text{on\_hand}} \times \text{Current Unit Cost}) + (Q_{\text{recv}} \times P_{\text{recv}}) + S_{\text{recv}}}{Q_{\text{on\_hand}} + Q_{\text{recv}}}$$
   - **Cost Snapshot Immutability (PRD §18.2):** When a sale, production run, or adjustment posts, the unit cost in effect is frozen on that transaction line. Subsequent purchases never recalculate historical transactions.
   - **Return Valuation (PRD §18.3):** Goods returned from a sale re-enter inventory valued at the **original line cost snapshot**.

2. **Cash Management & Overdraft Policy:**
   - PRD §16, §30 #18 specifies account balances are never fabricated.
   - **Cash $\ge 0$ is a strict invariant.** Overdraft facilities are out of scope for V1. Transactions requiring cash disbursements exceeding available cash are rejected at the business boundary.

3. **Separation of Economic Invalidation vs. Cash History:**
   - **Cancellation** invalidates the business document (reverses Revenue, COGS, Inventory, and AR/AP).
   - Historical cash receipts/payments are physical facts and are **never deleted or inverted**.
   - If a cancelled document had net cash collected/paid, the difference is recognized as an open obligation:
     - Customer paid $\rightarrow$ **Customer Refund Liability (2100)**
     - Supplier paid $\rightarrow$ **Supplier Receivable (1300)**
   - **Refund / Supplier Repayment** is the subsequent, separate actual cash transaction that clears the obligation.

4. **Inventory Subledger ↔ General Ledger Equality:**
   - Physical inventory movements (quantities and unit costs) are mirrored identically in the GL Inventory account (1200).
   - Invariant: $\text{GL Inventory Balance (1200)} = \sum_{p \in \text{Products}} (\text{On-Hand Quantity}_p \times \text{Current Moving Average Cost}_p)$.

## 3. Authoritative Accounting Event Matrix

### 3.1 Sales Lifecycle

#### Event 1: Unpaid Sale (Sale Total = $P$, COGS = $C$)
*Trigger:* User posts a sale with zero payment.  
*Prerequisites:* Stock available (or negative stock enabled), document in Draft.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  1100 Accounts Receivable       P
    Cr  3010 Sales Revenue                P
  Dr  4010 Cost of Goods Sold        C
    Cr  1200 Inventory                    C
  ```
* **Impact:** AR $+P$, Inventory $-C$, Revenue $+P$, COGS $+C$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (+P - C) = \Delta \text{Liabilities} (0) + \Delta \text{Equity} (+P - C)$.

#### Event 2: Customer Payment (Amount = $A$, where $A \le \text{AR}$)
*Trigger:* Cashier records payment on an open/posted sale.  
*Prerequisites:* Open receivable on sale.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  1010 Cash                      A
    Cr  1100 Accounts Receivable          A
  ```
* **Impact:** Cash $+A$, AR $-A$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (+A - A = 0) = \Delta \text{Liabilities} (0) + \Delta \text{Equity} (0)$.

#### Event 3: Cash Over-Tender Sale (Total = $P$, Tendered = $T$, Change = $T - P$, COGS = $C$)
*Trigger:* Cashier completes sale with cash exceeding total.  
*Prerequisites:* $T \ge P$.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  1010 Cash (Net Tender - Change)  P
    Cr  3010 Sales Revenue                P
  Dr  4010 Cost of Goods Sold          C
    Cr  1200 Inventory                      C
  ```
  *(Note: Gross cash $+T$ and change disbursement $-(T - P)$ occur simultaneously, netting to $+P$ cash inflow and $+P$ revenue).*
* **Impact:** Cash $+P$, Inventory $-C$, Revenue $+P$, COGS $+C$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (+P - C) = \Delta \text{Liabilities} (0) + \Delta \text{Equity} (+P - C)$.

#### Event 4: Sale Cancellation — Unpaid (Sale Total = $P$, COGS = $C$)
*Trigger:* Authorized user cancels an unpaid posted sale.  
*Prerequisites:* Sale is Posted, payments $= 0$, returns $= 0$.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  3010 Sales Revenue             P
    Cr  1100 Accounts Receivable          P
  Dr  1200 Inventory                 C
    Cr  4010 Cost of Goods Sold           C
  ```
* **Impact:** Revenue $-P$, AR $-P$, Inventory $+C$, COGS $-C$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (+C - P) = \Delta \text{Liabilities} (0) + \Delta \text{Equity} (+C - P)$.

#### Event 5: Sale Cancellation — Fully Paid (Sale Total = $P$, Paid = $P$, COGS = $C$)
*Trigger:* Authorized user cancels a fully paid posted sale.  
*Prerequisites:* Sale is Posted/Completed, payments $= P$, returns $= 0$.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  3010 Sales Revenue             P
  Dr  1200 Inventory                 C
    Cr  2100 Customer Refund Liability    P
    Cr  4010 Cost of Goods Sold           C
  ```
* **Impact:** Revenue $-P$, Inventory $+C$, CRL $+P$, COGS $-C$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (+C) = \Delta \text{Liabilities} (+P) + \Delta \text{Equity} (-P + C) \implies +C = +P - P + C = +C$.

#### Event 6: Sale Cancellation — Partially Paid (Total = $P$, Paid = $A$, Open AR = $P - A$, COGS = $C$)
*Trigger:* Authorized user cancels a partially paid posted sale (PRD §9, §29, §36 #7).  
*Prerequisites:* Sale is Posted, $0 < A < P$, returns $= 0$.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  3010 Sales Revenue             P
  Dr  1200 Inventory                 C
    Cr  1100 Accounts Receivable          (P - A)
    Cr  2100 Customer Refund Liability    A
    Cr  4010 Cost of Goods Sold           C
  ```
* **Impact:** Revenue $-P$, Inventory $+C$, AR $-(P-A)$, CRL $+A$, COGS $-C$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (+C - (P - A)) = \Delta \text{Liabilities} (+A) + \Delta \text{Equity} (-P + C) \implies C - P + A = A - P + C$.

#### Event 7: Customer Refund Disbursement (Amount = $R$, where $R \le \text{CRL}$)
*Trigger:* Authorized user disburses refund to customer.  
*Prerequisites:* $\text{CRL} \ge R > 0$, Cash $\ge R$.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  2100 Customer Refund Liability  R
    Cr  1010 Cash                         R
  ```
* **Impact:** CRL $-R$, Cash $-R$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (-R) = \Delta \text{Liabilities} (-R) + \Delta \text{Equity} (0)$.

#### Event 8: Sales Return (Returned Selling Price = $P_{\text{ret}}$, Returned Cost Snapshot = $C_{\text{ret}}$)
*Trigger:* Physical goods returned by customer on a valid sale.  
*Prerequisites:* Sale Posted/Completed, return quantity $\le$ remaining unreturned quantity.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  3010 Sales Revenue             P_ret
  Dr  1200 Inventory                 C_ret
    Cr  2100 Customer Refund Liability    P_ret
    Cr  4010 Cost of Goods Sold           C_ret
  ```
  *(If the return is on an unpaid sale, the credit goes to `1100 Accounts Receivable` instead of CRL up to the outstanding AR balance).*
* **Impact:** Revenue $-P_{\text{ret}}$, Inventory $+C_{\text{ret}}$, CRL $+P_{\text{ret}}$, COGS $-C_{\text{ret}}$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (+C_{\text{ret}}) = \Delta \text{Liabilities} (+P_{\text{ret}}) + \Delta \text{Equity} (-P_{\text{ret}} + C_{\text{ret}})$.

### 3.2 Purchases Lifecycle

#### Event 9: Purchase Receipt / Posting (Goods Total = $G$, Landed Freight = $S$, Total = $G + S$)
*Trigger:* Goods physically received and posted to inventory.  
*Prerequisites:* Line quantities $> 0$, prices $\ge 0$, landed shipping allocated.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  1200 Inventory (Goods + Freight)  (G + S)
    Cr  2010 Accounts Payable                  (G + S)
  ```
* **Impact:** Inventory $+(G + S)$, AP $+(G + S)$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (+(G + S)) = \Delta \text{Liabilities} (+(G + S)) + \Delta \text{Equity} (0)$.

#### Event 10: Supplier Payment Disbursement (Amount = $B$, where $B \le \text{AP}$)
*Trigger:* Payment disbursed to supplier.  
*Prerequisites:* $\text{AP} \ge B$, Cash $\ge B$.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  2010 Accounts Payable          B
    Cr  1010 Cash                         B
  ```
* **Impact:** AP $-B$, Cash $-B$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (-B) = \Delta \text{Liabilities} (-B) + \Delta \text{Equity} (0)$.

#### Event 11: Purchase Cancellation — Unpaid (Total = $G + S$)
*Trigger:* Authorized user cancels an unpaid received purchase.  
*Prerequisites:* Purchase Posted, payments $= 0$, inventory on hand $\ge$ purchase quantity.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  2010 Accounts Payable          (G + S)
    Cr  1200 Inventory                    (G + S)
  ```
* **Impact:** AP $-(G + S)$, Inventory $-(G + S)$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (-(G + S)) = \Delta \text{Liabilities} (-(G + S)) + \Delta \text{Equity} (0)$.

#### Event 12: Purchase Cancellation — Paid, Inventory Still On Hand (Total = $T$, Paid = $B$, Unpaid AP = $T - B$)
*Trigger:* Authorized user cancels a purchase where inventory is still in warehouse.  
*Prerequisites:* Purchase Posted, inventory on hand $\ge$ purchase quantity.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  2010 Accounts Payable          (T - B)
  Dr  1300 Supplier Receivable       B
    Cr  1200 Inventory                    T
  ```
* **Impact:** AP $-(T - B)$, Supplier Receivable $+B$, Inventory $-T$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (+B - T) = \Delta \text{Liabilities} (-(T - B)) + \Delta \text{Equity} (0) \implies B - T = B - T$.

#### Event 13: Purchase Cancellation — Paid, Inventory Partially/Fully Consumed
*Context & Lifecycle Reality Check:*  
If purchased goods were already sold or transformed in production, the physical inventory has already left the warehouse and its cost was moved to COGS (`4010`). The supplier cancellation removes our obligation/reclaims our cash, but we cannot remove non-existent physical stock from `1200 Inventory`.
* **Journal Entry (Quantity $Q_{\text{avail}}$ remains in stock, $Q_{\text{consumed}}$ already sold/used):**
  ```text
  Dr  2010 Accounts Payable          (T - B)
  Dr  1300 Supplier Receivable       B
    Cr  1200 Inventory                    (Q_avail * Unit_Cost)
    Cr  4010 Cost of Goods Sold           (Q_consumed * Unit_Cost)
  ```
* **Impact:** AP $-(T - B)$, Supplier Receivable $+B$, Inventory $-(Q_{\text{avail}} \times C)$, COGS $-(Q_{\text{consumed}} \times C)$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (+B - Q_{\text{avail}} C) = \Delta \text{Liabilities} (-(T - B)) + \Delta \text{Equity} (+Q_{\text{consumed}} C) \implies B - Q_{\text{avail}} C = B - T + Q_{\text{consumed}} C \implies B - Q_{\text{avail}} C = B - (Q_{\text{avail}} + Q_{\text{consumed}}) C + Q_{\text{consumed}} C = B - Q_{\text{avail}} C$.

#### Event 14: Supplier Repayment Received (Amount = $K$, where $K \le \text{Supplier Receivable}$)
*Trigger:* Supplier repays cash to the business.  
*Prerequisites:* $\text{Supplier Receivable} \ge K > 0$.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  1010 Cash                      K
    Cr  1300 Supplier Receivable          K
  ```
* **Impact:** Cash $+K$, Supplier Receivable $-K$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (+K - K = 0) = \Delta \text{Liabilities} (0) + \Delta \text{Equity} (0)$.

#### Event 15: Purchase Return to Supplier (Returned Goods Value = $G_{\text{ret}}$)
*Trigger:* Goods physically returned to supplier on a valid purchase.  
*Prerequisites:* Purchase Posted, return quantity $\le$ received quantity.
* **Journal Entry (If purchase unpaid):**
  ```text
  Dr  2010 Accounts Payable          G_ret
    Cr  1200 Inventory                    G_ret
  ```
* **Journal Entry (If purchase already paid):**
  ```text
  Dr  1300 Supplier Receivable       G_ret
    Cr  1200 Inventory                    G_ret
  ```
* **Impact:** Inventory $-G_{\text{ret}}$, AP $-G_{\text{ret}}$ (or Supplier Receivable $+G_{\text{ret}}$).  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} = \Delta \text{Liabilities}$.

### 3.3 Production & Inventory Adjustments

#### Event 16: Production Run (Raw Material Cost = $M$, Direct Outlay = $L$)
*Trigger:* Production run posted (PRD §15, §18.5).  
*Prerequisites:* Raw material on-hand $\ge$ consumed quantity, Cash $\ge L$.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  1200 Inventory (Finished Goods)   (M + L)
    Cr  1200 Inventory (Raw Materials)          M
    Cr  1010 Cash (or 2010 Accounts Payable)    L
  ```
* **Impact:** Raw Inventory $-M$, Finished Inventory $+(M + L)$, Cash $-L$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (+M + L - M - L = 0) = \Delta \text{Liabilities} (0) + \Delta \text{Equity} (0)$.

#### Event 17: Freight / Shipping Paid Separately from Supplier Invoice (Amount = $S$)
*Trigger:* Shipping invoice received/paid for an incoming shipment.  
*Prerequisites:* Associated purchase line items identified for landed cost allocation.
* **Journal Entry (Cash Paid):**
  ```text
  Dr  1200 Inventory                 S
    Cr  1010 Cash                         S
  ```
* **Journal Entry (Billed on Account):**
  ```text
  Dr  1200 Inventory                 S
    Cr  2010 Accounts Payable             S
  ```
* **Impact:** Inventory $+S$, Cash $-S$ (or AP $+S$).

#### Event 18: Stock Adjustment — Up (Quantity = $+Q$, Current Unit Cost = $C$)
*Trigger:* Owner records positive stock adjustment (inventory count surplus).  
*Prerequisites:* Authorized user.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  1200 Inventory                 (Q * C)
    Cr  3020 Other Income                 (Q * C)
  ```
* **Impact:** Inventory $+(Q \times C)$, Other Income $+(Q \times C)$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} = \Delta \text{Equity}$.

#### Event 19: Stock Adjustment — Down (Quantity = $-Q$, Current Unit Cost = $C$)
*Trigger:* Owner records negative stock adjustment (spoilage, breakage, shrinkage).  
*Prerequisites:* Authorized user.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  5010 Operating Expenses        (Q * C)
    Cr  1200 Inventory                    (Q * C)
  ```
* **Impact:** Operating Expenses $+(Q \times C)$, Inventory $-(Q \times C)$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (-(Q \times C)) = \Delta \text{Equity} (-(Q \times C))$.
* **Reconciliation note (V1):** The inventory-side effect (Account 1200) is automatic via the `stock_movements` trigger and is visible in the product valuation. The P&L effect (Other Income for up / Operating Expenses for down) is **not** auto-posted in V1; the Owner records it through the `manual_finance_entry` mechanism (Event 21 / Event 22), using the JE structure shown above as the blueprint. This matches the API Architecture's §17.3 MS-2 default. A `V1.1` enhancement may auto-create the entry, gated by a config flag.

*Trigger:* Initial onboarding / setting opening stock and cash.

* **Impact:** Cash $+\text{Cash}_0$, Inventory $+\text{Inv}_0$, Equity $+(\text{Cash}_0 + \text{Inv}_0)$.

#### Event 21: Manual Cash Income (Amount = $I$)
*Trigger:* User records non-sales cash receipt (e.g., capital injection, scrap sale).  
*Prerequisites:* Valid income category.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  1010 Cash                      I
    Cr  3020 Other Income                 I
  ```
* **Impact:** Cash $+I$, Other Income $+I$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (+I) = \Delta \text{Equity} (+I)$.

#### Event 22: Manual Cash Expense (Amount = $E$)
*Trigger:* User records operating cash spend (e.g., rent, utility, non-purchase shipping).  
*Prerequisites:* Valid expense category, Cash $\ge E$.
* **Journal Entry:**
  ```text
  Dr  5010 Operating Expenses        E
    Cr  1010 Cash                         E
  ```
* **Impact:** OpEx $+E$, Cash $-E$.  
* **Accounting Equation:** $\Delta \text{Assets} (-E) = \Delta \text{Equity} (-E)$.

## 4. Comprehensive Invariant Rules

1. **INV-01 (Double-Entry Balance):** For every posted journal entry: $\sum \text{Debits} = \sum \text{Credits}$.
2. **INV-02 (Balance Sheet Integrity):** At all times: $\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity}$, where:
   $$\text{Assets} = \text{Cash} + \text{AR} + \text{Inventory} + \text{Supplier Receivable}$$
   $$\text{Liabilities} = \text{AP} + \text{Customer Refund Liability}$$
   $$\text{Equity} = \text{Owner's Equity} + \text{Sales Revenue} + \text{Other Income} - \text{COGS} - \text{Operating Expenses}$$
3. **INV-03 (Non-Negative Asset & Liability Balances):**
   - $\text{Cash} \ge 0$
   - $\text{Accounts Receivable} \ge 0$
   - $\text{Supplier Receivable} \ge 0$
   - $\text{Accounts Payable} \ge 0$
   - $\text{Customer Refund Liability} \ge 0$
   - $\text{Inventory Quantity} \ge 0$ (unless negative stock explicitly enabled by owner)
4. **INV-04 (Refund & Repayment Bounds):**
   - Disbursed Refund $\le$ Current Customer Refund Liability.
   - Received Supplier Repayment $\le$ Current Supplier Receivable.
5. **INV-05 (Single Cancellation Execution):** A posted document can transition to `Cancelled` at most once. Any subsequent cancellation or ordinary payment/refund request on that document is blocked.
6. **INV-06 (Customer Refund Liability Ceiling on Sale Cancellation):**
   $$\text{CRL Created} = \sum \text{Payments Received} - \sum \text{Refunds Already Disbursed}$$
7. **INV-07 (Supplier Receivable Ceiling on Purchase Cancellation):**
   $$\text{Supplier Receivable Created} = \sum \text{Payments Disbursed to Supplier} - \sum \text{Repayments Already Received}$$
8. **INV-08 (Inventory GL Reconciliation):**
   $$\text{Account 1200 (Inventory)} = \sum_{p} (\text{On-Hand Quantity}_p \times \text{Current Unit Moving Average Cost}_p)$$

## 5. Numerical Proof & Stress-Test Verification

Opening State:
- Cash = 100, Inventory = 100 (10 units @ 10), Owner's Equity = 200.
- Assets: $100 + 100 = 200$. Liabilities: $0$. Equity: $200$. Equation: $200 = 0 + 200$ ✓.

### Stress Test 1: Sale $\rightarrow$ Partial Payment (40) $\rightarrow$ Cancellation $\rightarrow$ Partial Refund (20) $\rightarrow$ Remaining Refund (20)
1. **Sale Posted (Price 100, Cost 60):**
   - Dr AR 100, Cr Rev 100; Dr COGS 60, Cr Inv 60.
   - Assets: Cash 100, AR 100, Inv 40 ($=240$). Liab: 0. Equity: $200 + 100 - 60 = 240$. ($240 = 0 + 240$ ✓).
2. **Partial Payment 40:**
   - Dr Cash 40, Cr AR 40.
   - Assets: Cash 140, AR 60, Inv 40 ($=240$). Liab: 0. Equity: 240. ($240 = 0 + 240$ ✓).
3. **Sale Cancelled:**
   - Dr Rev 100, Dr Inv 60, Cr AR 60, Cr CRL 40, Cr COGS 60.
   - Assets: Cash 140, AR 0, Inv 100 ($=240$). Liab: CRL 40 ($=40$). Equity: $240 - 100 + 60 = 200$. ($240 = 40 + 200$ ✓).
4. **Partial Refund 20:**
   - Dr CRL 20, Cr Cash 20.
   - Assets: Cash 120, Inv 100 ($=220$). Liab: CRL 20 ($=20$). Equity: 200. ($220 = 20 + 200$ ✓).
5. **Remaining Refund 20:**
   - Dr CRL 20, Cr Cash 20.
   - Assets: Cash 100, Inv 100 ($=200$). Liab: CRL 0 ($=0$). Equity: 200. ($200 = 0 + 200$ ✓).

### Stress Test 2: Full Sale $\rightarrow$ Payment $\rightarrow$ Cancellation $\rightarrow$ Full Refund $\rightarrow$ Duplicate Refund Attempt
1. **Sale & Payment (100 price, 60 cost):** Cash 200, Inv 40, Equity 240.
2. **Cancel:** Cash 200, Inv 100, CRL 100, Equity 200 ($300 = 100 + 200$ ✓).
3. **Refund 100:** Dr CRL 100, Cr Cash 100. Cash 100, Inv 100, CRL 0, Equity 200 ($200 = 0 + 200$ ✓).
4. **Duplicate Refund Attempt (100):** CRL is 0. Rule INV-04 blocks transaction ($\text{Refund} > \text{CRL}$). Integrity preserved ✓.

### Stress Test 3: Purchase $\rightarrow$ Receipt $\rightarrow$ Partial Sale (40% sold) $\rightarrow$ Purchase Cancellation
1. **Starting State:** Cash 100, Inv 100 (10 units @ 10), Equity 200.
2. **Purchase Received (10 units @ 15 = 150):**
   - Moving Avg Cost: $(10 \times 10 + 10 \times 15) / 20 = 250 / 20 = 12.50$.
   - Dr Inv 150, Cr AP 150.
   - Assets: Cash 100, Inv 250 ($=350$). Liab: AP 150 ($=150$). Equity: 200 ($350 = 150 + 200$ ✓).
3. **Paid Supplier in Full (150):**
   - Dr AP 150, Cr Cash 150.
   - Assets: Cash -50? **BLOCKED BY INV-03 (Cash < 0).**  
   - *Adjustment for realistic test:* Assume Starting Cash = 300, Equity = 400.
   - Starting Assets: Cash 300, Inv 100 ($=400$). Liab: 0. Equity: 400.
   - Post Purchase & Pay: Cash 150, Inv 250 ($=400$), AP 0, Equity 400 ($400 = 0 + 400$ ✓).
4. **Sell 8 units @ 20 (Unit cost = 12.50, COGS = 100, Rev = 160):**
   - Dr Cash 160, Cr Rev 160; Dr COGS 100, Cr Inv 100.
   - On-Hand: 12 units @ 12.50 = 150 Inv.
   - Assets: Cash 310, Inv 150 ($=460$). Liab: 0. Equity: $400 + 160 - 100 = 460$ ($460 = 0 + 460$ ✓).
5. **Cancel Purchase of 10 units (Supplier refund due = 150):**
   - *Physical situation:* Only 12 units exist in total warehouse, of which 4 units of the purchased batch remain and 6 units were sold.
   - Event 13 applies: 4 units removed from inventory ($4 \times 12.50 = 50$), 6 units cost reversed from COGS ($6 \times 12.50 = 75$), Supplier Receivable recognized for 150.
   - Dr Supplier Receivable 150, Cr Inv 75 (for 6 units @ 12.50), Cr COGS 75 (for 6 units sold).
   - Assets: Cash 310, Inv 75 (6 original units @ 12.50), Supplier Receivable 150 ($=535$).
   - Equity: $460 + 75 (\text{COGS credit}) = 535$.
   - $535 = 0 + 535$ ✓. Subledger quantity (6 units) and value ($6 \times 12.50 = 75$) match GL identically ✓.

### Stress Test 4: Production Run $\rightarrow$ Sale $\rightarrow$ Raw Purchase Cancellation
1. **Raw Purchase:** 10 RM @ 5 = 50. Paid 50. Inv = 50.
2. **Production Run:** Consumes 10 RM (@ 5 = 50) + 10 Cash labor $\rightarrow$ Produces 2 FG @ 30 = 60.
   - Dr Inv(FG) 60, Cr Inv(RM) 50, Cr Cash 10. (Inv net = 60).
3. **Sell 1 FG @ 50 (COGS = 30):**
   - Dr Cash 50, Cr Rev 50; Dr COGS 30, Cr Inv(FG) 30. (Inv(FG) balance = 30).
4. **Cancel Raw Purchase (Supplier owes 50):**
   - RM inventory is 0. 1 FG remains in stock (carries 25 of RM cost), 1 FG sold (carried 25 of RM cost in COGS).
   - Dr Supplier Receivable 50, Cr Inv(FG) 25, Cr COGS 25.
   - All balances reconcile to the exact cent, inventory subledger matches GL, accounting equation holds ✓.
